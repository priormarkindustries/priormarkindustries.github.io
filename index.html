Our working principles

MAKE THE REQUIREMENTS CLEAR.

Quality starts by defining the product correctly. We encourage buyers to share specifications, references and intended use so the proposed product can be discussed against the right requirements.

  • ✓Agree on product details before order confirmation.
  • ✓Discuss sample needs and approval expectations where applicable.
  • ✓Clarify quantity, size breakdown, pricing and delivery arrangements.
  • ✓Keep communication open if any detail needs clarification.
Specific quality standards, tests, materials, lead times and shipping terms must be agreed for each project.
